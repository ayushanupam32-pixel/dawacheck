# NPPA ceiling prices, revised from 1 April 2026

Official orders of the National Pharmaceutical Pricing Authority (Ministry of Chemicals and
Fertilizers), published in the Gazette of India, Extraordinary, Part II Section 3(ii), on
25 March 2026, and listed on NPPA's "Circulars / Orders" page (https://nppa.gov.in/en/view_more?id=4)
on 27 March 2026.

- Downloaded: 2026-10-25, unchanged, by a plain request for each PDF (2 s apart). NPPA's web
  pages refuse scripts and its price dashboard uses rotating tokens, so neither is scripted;
  these PDF files are served to any visitor.
- sha256: nlem2022-767.pdf 8c974127f5518cf2a31eb1a7ea69fefa4fbb161ea3a58823e9a5d2587bf7897c
- sha256: nlem2015-138.pdf 5447f882902216c4ea63d23916a70f3ecd71c1466d83369c0f7dee3dc6c81a4d
- The prices are **exclusive of GST** ("ceiling prices exclusive of Goods and Services Tax
  applicable, if any").
- Not included: ceiling prices fixed by NPPA after 25.03.2026 for individual formulations (e.g.
  the order of 17.04.2026); these are few and are added when the next yearly order lists them.

Update yearly: when NPPA publishes the next April revision (late March), download the new orders
into pipeline/raw/nppa/<date>/ with a new SOURCE.md; parse_nppa.py reads the latest folder.
